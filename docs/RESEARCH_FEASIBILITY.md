# Research feasibility and data-source review

**Review date:** 2026-11-13  
**Status:** initial evidence-backed feasibility review; no source approved for acquisition.  
**Scope:** Turkish resumes versus Turkish backend software developer postings; junior and mid-level; multiple resumes ranked per posting. NDCG@5 is only a candidate primary metric, subject to feasibility.

## Executive assessment

Relevant methods and Turkish NLP references exist. The main feasibility risk is data: this review did not identify a verified, lawful, reusable Turkish backend resume collection paired with independently judged multi-resume lists per job. Public/commercial sources may supply job ads, but stated volume is not research permission and does not supply relevance judgments. A Turkish job-ad skill dataset may support skill-extraction research but is not a resume–job ranking benchmark.

Recommended primary route: a permissioned, bounded collection. Obtain job descriptions directly from employers or another data owner with written research/reuse permission; obtain Turkish resumes from volunteers under a reviewed notice/consent and privacy process; have independent evaluators judge anonymized resume–job pairs without model scores. This is conditional, not authorized or underway. If sufficient permissions or consented resumes cannot be obtained, pause or re-scope with the instructor. Do not scrape public boards.

## Reproducible multi-CV-per-job evaluation feasibility

**Not currently feasible on verified available data.** A defensible set requires rights to process full job and CV text for the research purpose; multiple eligible CVs for each job with documented provenance; human judgments against each specific posting made without model outputs; stable anonymized IDs and versioned snapshots; and enough candidate-list depth and relevance variation per job for NDCG@5 to be interpretable. This can only be assessed after authorized collection and a pilot.

Hiring outcomes are not required for human relevance ranking, but judgments remain subjective and must be described accordingly. Public or commercial postings alone supply neither resumes nor labels. Model-generated scores, synthetic resumes, inferred matches, and random pairing are not independent ground truth. Fictional examples may later demonstrate a UI, but cannot establish empirical ranking quality.

There is a construct-validity issue: volunteers’ CVs may not be actual applicants to each posting. Unless genuine application/pair provenance and permissions exist, describe the task as human-judged text relevance, not applicant suitability or actual hiring outcomes.

## Recommended collection strategy

**Primary, conditional:** obtain written permission for a bounded set of Turkish backend postings from an employer/data owner; recruit Turkish backend resumes under an institutionally reviewed information and consent process; remove direct identifiers before annotation; independently judge each resume against postings; keep private provenance records and publish only permitted, privacy-safe aggregates. Confirm institutional ethics/privacy review before recruitment or annotation. None of these permissions or approvals is currently obtained.

**Fallback:** if an employer will not authorize reuse, consider a vendor feed only after written confirmation of academic research, storage, quotation and publication rights, plus evidence of Turkish/backend/junior-mid coverage. Headcount Labs metadata may help identify employers and counts, but its published terms exclude descriptions. Göçmeden provides a licensed derived subset and summaries, not underlying full text; use only if the research question and permitted text are explicitly adjusted and instructor-approved. If neither path works, pause empirical ranking claims and request approval for a smaller consented sample or Turkish job-ad skill-extraction-only study. Do not scrape or substitute synthetic/proxy labels.
